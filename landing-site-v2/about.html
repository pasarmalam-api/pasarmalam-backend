Our business

Based in Cyberjaya, Selangor, we operate a marketplace connecting buyers and sellers in Malaysia for retail, bulk and wholesale purchases. PasarMalam brings together online shopping and opportunities for local businesses to reach consumers, resellers and business buyers.

We support participating sellers with shop onboarding, product presentation, digital promotion and physical promotional activities, according to the scope agreed with each seller. We aim to help businesses build visibility and develop distribution opportunities; sales and distributor appointments are not guaranteed.
