Delete your PasarMalam account

Tanitooluwa Ventures · Android app: com.pasarmalam.app

Request permanent deletion of your buyer or seller account and its associated personal data. You do not need to sign in or install the app to make a request.

Request account deletion

Email deletion request

Send your request from your registered email address to pasahmallam@gmail.com, with the subject PasarMalam account deletion request. Include your account name and registered email.

If the button does not open an email app, send the same request from Gmail or another email service. The request is only sent when you send the email.

If you cannot access your registered email, contact the same support address for help verifying ownership. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, card details or identity documents with your initial request.

Account and data covered

Your request covers your account, login access and associated personal data, including profile and contact details, saved addresses, cart and wishlist, messages and uploaded account content. For sellers, it also covers shop and branch profiles, listings, verification documents and payout details.

Buyer and seller modes can belong to the same account. State clearly if you only want to close your shop instead of deleting your whole account.

What happens next

Support reviews the request and verifies account ownership before deletion. You can submit a request even if you have outstanding orders, refunds or payouts; include their references so support can review them.

We complete account deletion and deletion of associated personal data within 30 days after verifying account ownership, except for records we are legally required to retain. Support will confirm completion by email.

Only financial records needed for tax and accounting obligations are retained for the applicable seven-year statutory period. This does not mean we keep your entire account for seven years. If another legal obligation requires specific records to be retained, support will explain which records, the reason and the applicable retention period. Retained records are restricted to those purposes and deleted when no longer required.

Requesting deletion does not itself cancel an order, issue a refund or delete an account immediately.

Uninstalling the app or signing out does not delete your account.
