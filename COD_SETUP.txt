PM EXPRESS COD PILOT

COD remains disabled unless PM_COD_ENABLED=true and all configuration below is
valid. No production settings were changed by this implementation.

Render backend environment variables:
PM_COD_MAX_TOTAL: approved maximum in MYR, including delivery, before vouchers.
PM_COD_AREAS: JSON list of [south_lat, west_lng, north_lat, east_lng] boxes.
PM_COD_VERIFIED_BUYERS: JSON object mapping buyer IDs to manually verified
Malaysian phone numbers in international format. Do not commit real customer
phone numbers to Git. Verify the account holder and delivery phone before
adding approval; changing either phone invalidates approval.

Example format ONLY, not approved production limits or service areas:
PM_COD_MAX_TOTAL=100
PM_COD_AREAS=[[2.90,101.60,3.00,101.70]]
PM_COD_VERIFIED_BUYERS={"123":"601112345678"}

Rules enforced again on the server when placing the order:
- Retail only, delivered by PM Express; bulk/factory remain Public Bank only.
- Valid server-priced delivery quotation and confirmed recipient coordinates.
- Both saved account phone and checkout phone match manual verification.
- At least one paid completed/delivered order; new customers prepay first.
- No previous unpaid COD order, including cancelled COD. Support must review
  exceptions; never mark an unpaid order paid just to restore eligibility.
- No parallel unpaid COD orders: buyer-level transaction locking.
- Remains unpaid until the existing admin cash-collection confirmation flow.

Before enabling, confirm the real cap and service boundaries, approve test
buyers, and test eligible/ineligible checkout with PM riders. A browser-visible
eligibility result never overrides the server's final check.
